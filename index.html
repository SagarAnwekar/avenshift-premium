HOW IT WORKS

One instruction in. Finished work out.

01

You give one instruction

Plain words, on WhatsApp or Telegram. "Build me a website." "Apply to these jobs." "Research this market." That is the whole job description.

02

The team plans and splits the work

The planner agent breaks it into tasks and hands them to specialist agents — research, build, QA — running in parallel, day and night.

03

Work is verified, then delivered

Every claim is checked against live sources. Every visual is screenshot-tested. You get the finished result with the receipts.

04

You approve the irreversible

Money, messages sent as you, deletions — the team drafts and waits for your yes. Everything else just gets done.
