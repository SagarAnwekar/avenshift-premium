Too fragmented

The dashboard person doesn't talk to the automation person. Nobody owns the whole outcome, so you end up managing the team yourself.

THE PROCESS

One instruction in. Finished work out.

01

You give one instruction

Plain words, on WhatsApp or Telegram. "Build me a website." "Automate these follow-ups." "Research this market." That is the whole job description.

02

The team plans and splits the work

The planner agent breaks it into tasks and hands them to specialist agents — research, build, QA — running in parallel, day and night.

03

Work is verified, then delivered

Every claim is checked against live sources. Every visual is screenshot-tested. You get the finished result with the receipts.

04

You approve the irreversible

Money, messages sent as you, deletions — the team drafts and waits for your yes. Everything else just gets done.

FAQ

Asked often.

Do I need to be technical to use this?

No. You talk in plain words — Hindi, English or Hinglish — on WhatsApp or Telegram. The planner agent turns it into a technical plan. You never see a line of code unless you ask.

Can an agent really replace an IT hire?

For research, dashboards, automation, drafts, monitoring and first-line support — yes, today. For judgment calls and final approvals, the human boss stays in charge. Think 70-80% of the daily grind, gone.

Will agents act without my permission?

They work independently on research and drafts, but anything irreversible — spending money, sending a message as you, deleting something — waits for your explicit yes. That rule is built in, not a promise.

What does it cost?

We are in early access and onboarding pilot businesses personally. Pricing is being finalised — talk to us and early partners get founder terms.

Is my business data safe?

Secrets live in an encrypted vault, agents follow least-privilege access, and a dedicated security watch flags suspicious instructions — including ones disguised as coming from you.

Which languages do the agents speak?

English, Hindi, Marathi and natural Hinglish — typed or spoken. Vaani BI already takes spoken sales in Marathi and replies by voice.
